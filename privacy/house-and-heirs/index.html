House & Heirs Privacy Policy

Effective November 13, 2026

This policy applies to the iOS and Android versions of House & Heirs published by Apps Bay.

Data the game stores

The game stores your progress, backup saves, and settings on your device so you can continue playing. A save may contain a house surname or other text you enter in the game. You can copy a save to the device clipboard and paste a save into the game. Exporting a save puts it on your clipboard so you can choose where to keep or share it. Apps Bay does not receive copies of your saves. Your operating system may include app data in device backups, depending on your settings.

Data collection and sharing

House & Heirs runs offline and has no account, advertising, analytics, in-app purchases, or developer-operated crash reporting. The game does not send player data to Apps Bay or share it with other companies. When you open the privacy policy, your browser loads this website separately.

Retention and deletion

Your local game data remains on your device while the game stores it there. You can delete individual backups in House Archives. To remove all local game data, delete the app or clear its storage in system settings where available. Manage exported copies, clipboard contents, and any device backups separately.

Contact

If you email Apps Bay about the game or your privacy, we receive the email address and information you choose to send and use it to respond. You can contact us at appsbayarea@gmail.com.
