Privacy policy for 民国文学 on Android

Package: com.appsbay.minguoliteratural · Effective 2 October 2026

This page describes the Android app 民国文学, published on Google Play by Ida yang (the same developer as Apps Bay). It covers app version 1.3.0 and later. For earlier versions, see our general privacy policy.

What the app stores

The books and covers are included with the app. Your favorites, reading progress, display and language preferences, advertising choice, and ad-free purchase status are stored on your device. We do not require an account or operate a reading-data server. Android backup may retain some local settings if you enable device backup.

Advertising and your choice

When European consent rules apply, Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) presents the regional consent form. Its choices control whether the app may request ads; where permitted, ads may still be shown without personalization. Google UMP may process technical information needed to determine regional requirements and record your choice.

In other regions, the app presents two unselected options: Allow ads and data sharing or Don’t sell or share my data. If you choose the latter or dismiss the screen, the app continues without ad requests. This choice does not restrict reading features. You may change it at any time using More → Privacy settings. If that entry is unavailable in your installed version, contact us at the email below.

If you allow ads, Google AdMob and its advertising partners may receive your advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and interactions with ads to select, deliver, measure, and protect advertising. Google’s data handling is described in the Google Privacy Policy. We do not sell personal information for money. Under some US state laws, using advertising data for cross-context advertising can count as a “sale” or “sharing”; the in-app no-sharing choice prevents ad requests.

To prevent an ad request while you change your choice, the app hides visible ads and clears ads it preloaded. A choice to stop data sharing is applied to future ad requests. You can also manage your Android advertising ID in your device’s privacy settings.

Purchases and feedback

The optional “Remove ads” purchase uses Google Play Billing. Google processes the payment and purchase information under its own terms and privacy policy. The app checks purchase status with Google Play and stores whether ad-free access is active on your device. We do not receive your payment card details.

The Feedback button opens your email app. We receive your email address and message only if you choose to send the email, and use them to respond to you.

How to request data deletion

To request deletion of data held by Apps Bay for this app, email appsbayarea@gmail.com with the subject “Minguo Literature Android data deletion.” Tell us which data you want deleted and the email address you used to contact us. We may ask you to verify that address before deleting correspondence.

Reading progress, favorites, settings, and advertising choices are stored on your device rather than an Apps Bay server. You can delete them immediately by clearing the app’s storage in Android settings or uninstalling the app; a device backup may retain a copy under your Android backup settings. We delete developer-held feedback emails and related correspondence within 30 days of a verified deletion request, unless a legal obligation requires us to keep them longer. Google Play purchase records and data held by Google or its advertising partners are governed by their own retention and deletion processes; use their privacy controls or contact them directly.

For access, correction, or other rights available under your local law, email the same address. You can change advertising choices under More → Privacy settings.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the app or its providers change. The effective date above will change when we publish a revision. Questions and requests can be sent to appsbayarea@gmail.com.
